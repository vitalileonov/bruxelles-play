FUCKING IN BRUXELLES

RU EN

🔥 Project Description

FUCKING IN BRUXELLES is an independent theatrical production at the intersection of documentary performance, poetry, and video art. Based on the play by Mikita Ilyinchyk and directed by Agnius Jankevicius (Lithuania), it tells the story of a Belarusian protester forced to flee the country due to political persecution.

Settling across the street from the European Parliament in Brussels, the protagonist dreams of punishing European indifference. But what awaits him there—salvation or madness? The answer is left to the audience.

🎯 Goals

  • Create a bold, socially relevant theater performance
  • Unite Belarusian and Lithuanian artists
  • Film and release an online version of the show
  • Give a voice to those who’ve been silenced
