📖 About the Project

“FUCKING IN BRUXELLES” is an independent theatre production blending documentary theatre, poetry, and psychedelic video art. It's the story of a man who lived through protests, fear, and exile — now residing directly across from the European Parliament. Not by chance. He believes: Europe betrayed.

The protagonist is a Belarusian forced to flee after the protests. He was persecuted. They tried to break him. He ended up in Brussels — in the heart of Europe — and now his mission is to "punish the European Parliament." Because Europe saw. And remained silent.

This is not a play about politics. It's a play about pain, loneliness, and resistance. A scream in a world that’s gone deaf. A raw story of being a nobody in a foreign land, where your fear isn’t recognized as real.
