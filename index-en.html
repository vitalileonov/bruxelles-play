FUCKING IN BRUXELLES

An independent theater production

🎭 A Modern Play About Freedom, Justice, and Loneliness in Europe

📖 About the Project

"FUCKING IN BRUXELLES" is a theatrical performance at the intersection of documentary theater, poetry, and psychedelic video art. Based on the play by Mikita Ilyinchyk, directed by Agnius Jankavičius (Lithuania). This is the story of a Belarusian forced to flee his country due to political persecution. A man whose pain turned him into a weapon, and his thirst for justice into obsession. His goal — to punish the European Parliament. But how his path ends — is not up to him. The audience will see.

🎯 Our Goal

  • To produce a bold, honest, social performance
  • To talk with the audience about pain, exile, loss of home, and revenge
  • To unite Belarusian and Lithuanian actors, artists, and composers
  • To create an online version of the performance
  • To give a stage to voices that are not being heard

💰 How Funds Will Be Used

  • Rehearsals and team fees
  • Set design, costumes, lighting
  • Sound design and original music
  • Theater rental in Vilnius
  • Professional filming and editing
  • Promotion of the project in Europe
